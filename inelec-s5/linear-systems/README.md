## Syllabus checklist

- [ ] Discrete-time signals and systems (properties, interconnection)
- [ ] Discrete LTI systems: impulse response, convolution sum, graphical convolution
- [ ] Fourier series of discrete periodic signals
- [ ] DTFT and DFT
- [ ] Z-transform: inverse, properties, unilateral
- [ ] Sampling: theorem, aliasing, reconstruction
- [ ] Discrete-time models: zero-order hold, transfer function, state space
